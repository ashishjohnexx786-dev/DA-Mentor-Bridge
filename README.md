## Study flow

Study the mapped lesson/source, do the engineering task yourself, save evidence, validate the result and use protected review only after a genuine attempt. Repair weaknesses and complete the changed/fresh retry required by the Gate state.

Course 3 entry is not a percentage shortcut: all six bridge phases, the B06 capstone, the Final Gate and every required readiness floor must be satisfied. A weak critical area cannot be averaged away.

The Mentor records your study decisions and evidence. It does not automatically grade competence and does not replace SQL, Python, warehouse, cloud/platform or other native execution evidence.

AMOLED and light themes, adjustable reading size and an optional stopwatch are available in Settings. Export progress to transfer between devices; phone and PC do not synchronize automatically. Videos require access to their original provider. Browser-cached pages/assets may remain available offline after they have been visited; large repository/course downloads use the network.

Download the current repository/course package from Settings. Read VERIFICATION.md for static checks and native-runtime limits. No generated video lessons are included.
